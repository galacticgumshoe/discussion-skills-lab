In Opening Stage, you learned that the round begins when the task is issued under the tournament’s procedure. Now consider what you can do before that moment. Ask your coach how tasks will be distributed, because preparation differs when the task is available early.

### Electronically

Electronic publication may involve a shared online document. Open the announced document as soon as it is available and monitor updates throughout the day. If a task is released early, use the permitted advance time to analyze it and organize relevant research. Stay flexible about the group’s decisions.

### On paper

For paper tasks, a judge or tournament official passes copies face-down to every competitor and the judge. At State, this is the Room Manager; some invitational tournaments also have an official bring tasks to every table.

At State, the Room Manager reads the task aloud, at which point the round begins. At other tournaments, students choose when to flip over their tasks to begin the round. Have your materials ready so you can read and analyze promptly.
