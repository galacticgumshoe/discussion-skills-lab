## Equipment for the round

- **A fully charged internet-capable device** for research, such as a tablet, laptop, or Chromebook. Bring a charger as backup.
- **A readable name tent** in front of your device so others can address you by name. Fold a sheet of printer paper into a triangle, and write your name in large script on one of the sides of the triangle. Pronouns or a speaker code are optional.
- **Something to take notes with:** a pen or pencil and notebook, for example.
- **A refillable water bottle.** A tournament is a long day with lots of talking; stay hydrated.
- **A phone/hotspot backup, if available.** A hotspot is useful when school Wi-Fi is unreliable, but it is **not required**. Bring a phone charger if using it.

## Plan for the tournament day

Snacks, lunch if desired, and warm layers can help between rounds. Concession lines, dietary needs, and cost may make bringing lunch useful; check the schedule for meal time. Buses and buildings can be cold, so consider clothing you can wear over your speech attire between rounds.

It is highly recommended to avoid bringing food with you to the round. Try timing your meals and snacks for between rounds. Bringing a sealable water bottle with you to the round is acceptable. These are not official rules, but recommendations to minimize messes and potential distractions during a round of competition.
