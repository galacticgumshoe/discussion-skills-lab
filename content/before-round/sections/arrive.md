Find your assigned room or table early enough to get settled before the round starts.

### Orient yourself

Rounds may take place at tables in a gymnasium or in classrooms. Give yourself time to find the location and check the round’s seating instructions.

For invitational tournaments, seating is optional and handled in a first-come, first-serve fashion. Meaning the first person to arrive gets to pick where they sit. It is advantageous to arrive early enough that you can try to arrange where the judge will sit, and place yourself as close to them as possible. Having a direct sight line to the judge as well as being close so you are easier to hear are the primary goals for seating yourself.

For MSHSL tournaments (Sections, State), seating order is determined from your speaking order shown on the ballot/schematic. The speaker in first position would be in the left-most seat, followed in order until the last speaker would be in the right-most seat (from the judge's perspective).

### Set up and connect

Place your readable name tent and materials on the table and get comfortable. Test internet access before the round; if needed, troubleshoot promptly and use an available, approved hotspot or other backup. A hotspot is useful, not mandatory.
