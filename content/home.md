# Build the skills for a stronger Discussion round.

Three interactive lessons guide you through opening a round, contributing during the discussion, and bringing the group's work to a purposeful conclusion. Practice at your own pace, revisit activities, and use each lesson's Quick Reference when you need it.

**About your progress:** Each lesson saves progress in this browser on this device. It does not automatically sync to another device or to your coach. The Quick Reference in each lesson is available at any time.

Educational coaching materials are not a substitute for official tournament rules.
