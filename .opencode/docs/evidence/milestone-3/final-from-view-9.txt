Final Stage Quick Reference
01 Summary vs. synthesis
Summary: Organize and accurately restate findings, proposals, agreements, and unresolved questions.
Synthesis: Connect viewpoints to develop a new insight or recommendation.
Remember: Summary captures what the group developed. Synthesis advances its thinking.

03 Effective summaries
Organize by theme, not speaking order. Include barriers, important evidence and proposals, agreed recommendations, unresolved
concerns, and credit for substantive contributions.
Try: “Our group identified three major considerations…” · “We agreed to recommend…” · “One concern that remains unresolved is…”
04 Consensus and task completion
Do not confuse majority support with unanimity. Identify remaining concerns, explore revisions, and confirm whether the revised
outcome is supportable.
Check the WHAT (the problem) and HOW (required deliverable components). Does the final product meet the task? Can
